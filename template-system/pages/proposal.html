Payment retry design

Preserve the original payment key after a timeout or worker crash. Do not issue a new charge until the original outcome is resolved.

Draft · sampleBug fix · Provider contract unverified
Proposed decision

Store one immutable key per payment. Reconcile unknown outcomes; retry only within the provider’s verified guarantees.

{{icon:contract}}Requirements

Required behavior

A crash after a successful charge must not cause a new logical charge. Existing operations retain their original keys.

Outside this decision

No payment-provider migration, billing redesign or task breakdown. Throughput tuning follows measurement.
