Payment retry design

Keep the original operation identity through every recovery path. An illustrative bug-fix proposal, not a recommendation for an unidentified payment provider.

Draft exampleType · Bug fix
Proposed decision

Use a durable, immutable payment key and reconcile ambiguous outcomes within verified provider guarantees.

Requirements

Required behavior

A crash after a successful charge must not cause a new logical charge. Existing operations retain their original keys.

Outside this decision

No payment-provider migration, billing redesign or task breakdown. Throughput tuning follows measurement.
