# Architecture decision recipe

Use for a bounded choice, not an implementation plan.

## Decision and context

State the recommendation, status and the constraint driving the choice. List only assumptions that can change the result. A spike with insufficient evidence may end in a conditional recommendation and a bounded experiment rather than a false decision.

## Evaluation

| Criterion | Existing approach | Candidate | Evidence / uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Replace with a load-bearing requirement | Observed behavior | Proposed behavior | Source, test or unknown |

Add columns only for credible options and rows only for meaningful criteria. Do not manufacture weighted scores, costs or benchmark numbers. Separate team preference from mandatory compatibility/security constraints.

## Consequences and revisit trigger

Record benefits, accepted costs, rejected alternatives, the counterexample most likely to invalidate the choice, and a concrete revisit condition. Link superseded decisions if verified. End with the minimum evidence needed to accept the proposal.
