# Organization engineering standards

Private binding contract; no organization facts or rubric are bundled. Load only for engineering work within the source's declared organization and project scope.

Record the user-approved operating standard, original rubric or decision references, applicability, source date/revision and unresolved facts. Separate desired impact and performance targets from observed results. Include relevant delivery and organizational-leverage measures, ownership and feedback expectations without manufacturing baselines, scores or private product facts.

Keep the general engineering-effectiveness policy authoritative for workflow boundaries. Store only organization-specific interpretation and preferences here; do not repeat general implementation, review or testing procedures. A supplied summary is not proof that the original rubric was independently read or that the target level was achieved. Preserve a recoverable prior version when refreshing this source.
